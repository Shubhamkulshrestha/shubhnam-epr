About Shubhnam EPR

Learn about Shubhnam EPR, an independent plastic EPR facilitation business connecting brands and recycling partners in India.

Make EPR coordination simpler

Shubhnam EPR is an independent facilitation and coordination business. We connect companies seeking plastic waste processing support with recycling partners, help gather relevant documentation, and support communication through the engagement.

Support for both sides of the partnership

For brands

Share your requirement once. We coordinate partner shortlisting, document collection, commercial discussions, agreed milestones and completion follow-ups through one point of contact.

Discuss your recycling requirements →

For recyclers

Connect with relevant brand enquiries, receive clearer material and quantity requirements, and get support coordinating proposals, documents and agreed timelines. Each engagement is subject to mutual agreement.

Enquire about becoming a partner →

Know the next step

We help both parties define deliverables, responsibilities and timelines upfront, track progress and follow up on outstanding documentation. Recycling and certificate obligations remain with the responsible parties.
